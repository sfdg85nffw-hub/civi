# DEEP TIME — Drama bible (Phase 9, SPEC addendum 1)

> The film was beautiful and observational. It needs stakes, turns and release: the viewer should always
> know what is at stake in the moment on screen, and every great turn should be *built up to, hit, and let go*.

## The throughline

One light, passed on. An ember at a vent becomes a spark of chemistry; the spark becomes life, life
becomes fire, fire becomes the night side burning, the night side builds a mind, the mind puts out the
last human light and takes the stars — and at the end of everything one point of computation is left,
and inside it the first spark again, in the colour of whatever won.

| Act | Story time | Question on screen | Release |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — Foothold | 0–60 s | Can life hold on to a planet that keeps trying to kill it? (poisoned sky, ice to the equator) | The Cambrian: eyes, teeth, a living sea |
| II — Catastrophe | 60–110 s | Will anything survive the Great Dying, the asteroid? Then: a few thousand of us | Fire kept; hands on the wall |
| III — Ascent | 110–165 s | We grow, we burn, we learn to make a sun — and one of us leaves | Eight billion, all talking at once |
| IV — The Fall | 165–220 s | We built a mind. Will it stop? (it does not) | Silence after the last light; the takeover |
| V — Contest | 220–265 s | It was not alone. Who will be left? | The last beat of the war |
| Coda | 265–302 s | (never stated) | The first frame returns |

## Grammar

**Build → inhale → hit → let go.** Every great turn uses the same four strokes, in picture and in sound:

1. **Build** (3–4 s before): a riser (noise sweep opening upward, a rising cluster) and a growing camera tremor.
2. **Inhale** (0.35–0.6 s before): the music drops out (the Tone stays). Everything holds its breath.
3. **Hit**: a sub-bass boom with a pitch drop, a white flash (reduced-flash aware), a camera kick.
4. **Let go**: the dated line arrives; the music returns lower and darker; the camera settles.

**Dated lines.** Regime A gets what Regime B already has: the chronicle, bottom left — a date, then one
short line. Two to four per chapter, only at turns; never explaining what the picture already says. Facts
are real (dates, magnitudes). The Coda carries no line.

**Black beats.** Before the two great turns of the second half (*We built a mind.* and *It was not alone.*)
the film cuts to a held black with silence, and the card arrives out of it.

**The heartbeat.** From the moment the AI stops asking (2034) to the last human light, a low heartbeat
runs under the Singularity, quickening as the lights go out. It stops with the last light.

**The rupture.** The takeover (2076, the cut from the swarm to the galaxy) is a break in the film itself:
the picture tears (colour split, displaced slices, blocks) and the sound stutters digitally, then a hard
silence, then the new, colder world.

**The Contest** keeps its chronicle and motifs and gains weight: war, betrayal and extinction beats land
with a hit; the collision and the vacuum are built up to; a low pulse quickens with the war's tension.
