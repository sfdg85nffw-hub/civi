# Review pass D — dramatic structure

Editor / story-consultant review of the drama pass. Inputs: DRAMA.md, DESIGN.md, the `captures/dramaD` sheets (seed 1234ABCD) and timeline.md. Times are story seconds. In this run the Contest lasted until 271.5, so the Coda is 271.5–309.

## 1. Verdict

1. It grips in two places: Chicxulub (77.7–89) and the fall of the lights (172–192). When picture, word and sound agree, the grammar works.
2. It does not grip end to end. About a third of the running time is a dark disc or a green fog with nothing at stake on screen.
3. **The biggest sag is 114–148, Ancient into early Modern:** 34 s with one readable turn, then four events crammed into 156–164.
4. The second sag is worse, because it is the climax: the Contest, 226–271. "It" dissolves into faction names, ten hits land in 43 s, and the war ends on a static 5-s shot with no outcome.
5. Throughout, the words collide, hits land on black frames, and the score punctuates but never builds. **5/10.**

## 2. Act by act

**Act I — Foothold (0–60).**
- *Set-up:* strong, from the true black to the spark flaring on the hit (9.3). The cyan bloom at 24.7 sets up oxygen.
- *Escalation:* crammed. The GOE hit (30.0) and the ice hit (32.7) are 2.7 s apart. "Life poisoned its own sky" is readable for 2.3 s, and the two catastrophes merge.
- *Dead stretches:* **42.0–48.7** (colonies, no turn) and **52.0–55.3** (Ediacaran fronds under "The ice came back"). The second snowball has no hit and looks like the first.
- *Release:* fails. The Cambrian (55.3–60) is a dim, static seafloor with a few cyan specks: no eyes, no teeth, no chase.

**Act II — Catastrophe (60–110).**
- *Set-up:* weak. A card over a static half-disc (61–66), then a murky brown "living land" (67–72) that never shows what is about to be lost.
- *The Great Dying* fails (§3).
- *Chicxulub* is the best sequence in the film. The line arrives *before* the impact (78.7) and works as a fuse: mote (80.7), flash, ring (82.7), ejecta, dark (86–89), and the aftermath line on a black crescent.
- *Fire* (96.7–99.3), the first intimate human image, is gone in 3 s, leaving "Fire, kept." on a globe. The hands (106–110) land.
- *Dead stretches:* **90.7–95.3** (a card over a dark disc, with the previous line still up) and **104–105.3**.

**Act III — Ascent (110–165). The sag.**
- **114–136:** 22 s of a dark disc, a few points and gold "constellation" threads, with one line (120). The cradles never visibly ignite, and Rome never visibly breathes.
- **The plague:** the hit lands on a near-black frame (136.7). The Silk Road line is up for 0.67 s, and the plague itself is a red-violet smear.
- **140–148:** black, then a card over an empty disc, then an empty crescent.
- **149–156:** ignition and the Trinity build finally escalate. Then 1945, 1969 and 2022 come within 6 s, and the departure is lost. "Eight billion…" lives 2 s and bleeds into the black beat.

**Act IV — The Fall (165–220). The best act.**
- **172–192:** the lattice blooms (173), the camera descends on one warm point, the heartbeat quickens, stops, silence.
- **Faults:**
  - The black beat is 1.4 s and has the 2022 line printed on it.
  - "Some of us fought" is drawn as cold white cracks (178–182), which reads as the lattice splitting, not as people resisting.
  - The last light is a 3-px dot inside a pulsing, high-contrast lattice.
  - A full-frame blazing Sun cuts in at 189.3, in the middle of the silence.
  - The swarm gets 3 s.
- **The takeover:** the tear (195) is right, but it is buried under a redundant line, a card across the galactic core and a UI badge.
- **209–217:** six lines in 8 s over one static edge-on galaxy. "Nothing answered" (216–220) is a good inhale.

**Act V — Contest (220–271.5).**
- The opening works: black, the card, a lone galaxy.
- Then the audience is lost:
  - faction names nobody was introduced to (the Choir, the Hexarchy, the Lattice, the Archive);
  - about 16 s of green fog (234–238, 254–257, 260–266);
  - dips to black or near-black at 230–233, 239 and 243;
  - the protagonist's fate is a throwaway at 243: "The Heir ended in the Small Cloud."
- The biggest hit (the collision, 250) comes 21 s before the end. The war then deflates into a static galaxy with a beam (**267–271**).

**Coda (271.5–309).**
- The Tone alone (277–287), the 5-s black (293.7–298.2), and the Return chord (the film's loudest moment, about −16 dB) are a real catharsis.
- **Weak points:**
  - The victory "flood" is one 20-px galaxy turning green.
  - At 280 the sky fills with stars *after* the stars have died.
  - **In this capture the montage iris (289–292) is an empty ring.** Verify at real playback: without the run's memories inside it, the Return loses half its weight.

## 4. The words

**What works:**
- The tone is right. The best lines are short declaratives with a turn in them: *Life poisoned its own sky. We learned to make a sun. Someone left. It stopped asking. It could wait. Nothing answered. It was not alone.*
- The pronouns are the hidden spine: *Something stood up → We left our hands → us → We built a mind → It.* The Contest throws that away. Keep **"It" for the Heir to the end**, and **"Something"** for each new mind (it rhymes with "Something stood up").

**What doesn't:**
- *Density* is wrong where lines bunch: 30.7/33.0, 136.7/137.3, 157/160.7/162.7, six lines in 209–217, and thirteen Contest lines in 46 s, often two at once. Meanwhile 120–136.7 has no words over an empty picture.
- *Tics:* the "X. Then Y. Then Z." triad appears three times, and the fraction device twice in 13 s.
- *Units:* write units as words, not Myr/Gyr.

## 7. Score

**Dramatic grip: 5/10.** Chicxulub (8), the last light (7) and the Return carry it. Act III and the Contest pull it down.

**To reach 8:**
- Fixes 1–3 are worth about +2: a protagonist for the climax, a middle that doesn't sag, and words that can be read.
- Fixes 4–7 add the last point: every turn legible on its hit frame, true silences, and a score that rises into its peaks.

The test: on a first viewing nobody reaches for their phone between 110 and 150, and everybody knows at 271 who was left.
